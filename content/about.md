I’m a software engineer who enjoys building things that probably didn’t need to be built from scratch… but somehow ended up being built from scratch anyway.

Most of my work revolves around backend systems, distributed services, and performance-focused applications. My primary language is Go, and I’m particularly interested in APIs, microservices, databases, authentication, and the engineering problems that make you open ten browser tabs and question your life choices.

Outside of coding, you’ll usually find me reading about distributed systems, experimenting with new technologies, digging into software architecture, or turning a random idea into a side project just to see how far I can take it.

I’m especially interested in understanding how things work under the hood — from designing reliable services to figuring out why something is suddenly 300ms slower than it was yesterday.

I also consume an unreasonable amount of coffee ☕ and have a growing collection of side projects that are, technically speaking, under control.

If you’re building something interesting, have a weird technical problem to solve, or just want to talk about software or startups, feel free to say hi.
